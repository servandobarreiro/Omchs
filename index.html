Cyan jacks are outputs, violet jacks are inputs. Patch from output → input (inputs stay dim until you drag from an output). On phones: tap an output, then tap an input. Click the dot on a cable for its attenuator, double-click the dot (or click the cable itself) to remove it.

Osc B is independent of Osc A. Set Osc B→A Mode to Mix and use Amt / Xfade to crossfade A↔B in the main path; other modes (FM / AM / Ring / Fold / Sync) are modulation, not a blend.

Commands (type into the field by the scope, or use the keyboard on desktop): 1-0 play a preset, ed3 arm/exit edit mode on preset 3, xy toggle the Lissajous scope, rd randomize, xprt start/stop recording (16-bit wav, or 24-bit in hq), hq toggle high-quality audio, mic toggle microphone input, n3name name preset 3, psave/pload/pxprt/pimprt persist or export/import presets, fllp/flhp/flbp/flnt/floff set Filter2's mode, icons toggle icon/text labels.

Sequencer: sq status, sqplay/sqstop, sqmode pitch|trig, bpm120, sq n c4 e4 . g4 notes, sq d 8 8 4 durations (4=quarter, 8=eighth, 16=16th), sq p 1 1 3 presets (values only), sq e 5 8 Euclidean, sqdiv16 sqlen16 sqclear sqshow. Saved with presets.

MIC arms a low-latency external input (echo cancel / AGC off). Patch the Mic out jack anywhere, or feed Ext In to run the mic through the filter / fold / delay / FX path.

Serve this folder over HTTP (e.g. python3 -m http.server) so AudioWorklets can load from omchs-worklets.js. Mic also needs HTTPS or localhost.
